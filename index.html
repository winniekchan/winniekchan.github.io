About me

Hello

I'm a Senior Analyst at Kepler Group, where I've spent the past four years working on paid search, pharma, and B2B clients like Spotify, Sanofi, and DoorDash. I like figuring out which campaigns are actually working, whether that means building Datorama dashboards, bringing in multi-touch attribution data, or setting up an incrementality test.

I also like helping people grow. I've led analysts on my teams and trained a cohort of 33 new grads and career-changers in digital marketing fundamentals. Lately I've been using AI tools like Gemini to speed up reporting and data pulls, which leaves more time for the analysis.
